Pick your hood (any spot in it, not your home address) and get 10 questions about it.

        How Aroundle works

        1. Pick your hood: search for a street, park or school, or use your location (never your home address).
        2. Get 10 questions about the 1 km around it, built from open data: streets, schools, shops, public transport, history and nature.
        3. Answer fast for more points, then dare a friend with the same questions and climb the leaderboards.
        Is Aroundle free?

        Yes. It is a free, non-commercial, open-source game (MIT licence), without accounts or ads.

        Where do the questions come from?

        From templates over open data — OpenStreetMap, Overture Maps, Wikipedia and Wikidata, GBIF, Transitous and Open-Meteo. No AI writes the questions, and only facts found in the sources are asked.

        Does it track my location?

        No. Your spot is moved 50 m in a random direction before anything is sent to the data sources. There are no accounts; answers are recorded anonymously, without names or exact location.

        How often can I play?

        One quiz per neighbourhood per day — a new one comes tomorrow. You can play as many different neighbourhoods as you like.
